Alongside my personal projects, I am a maintainer of the official Redux
libraries. Redux Toolkit deserves its own entry here rather than being
presented as a project I built alone.

## Building with other people

Open source is as much about conversations, clear explanations, and careful
tradeoffs as it is about code.

This draft is a place to describe specific contributions and the lessons
behind them, once the examples and wording have been chosen.

## Useful starting points

The official documentation and repository are linked above. They are the best
places to explore the library itself; this page will eventually tell the more
personal story of contributing to it.
