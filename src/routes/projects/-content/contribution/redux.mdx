After collaborating with the Redux maintainers and opening <a href="https://github.com/reduxjs/redux-toolkit/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3AEskiMojo14" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">quite a few PRs</a> for new features, I was invited to join as a co-maintainer in March 2023.

I was able to contribute heavily towards the Redux v5 and RTK v2 releases, implementing new features such as:

- [Action creator middleware](https://redux.js.org/toolkit/api/actionCreatorMiddleware)
- [Dynamic middleware](https://redux.js.org/toolkit/api/createDynamicMiddleware)
- [Async thunks within `createSlice`](https://redux.js.org/toolkit/api/createSlice#the-reducers-creator-callback-notation)
- [Slice selectors](https://redux.js.org/toolkit/api/createSlice#selectors)
- [Lazy loading slices](https://redux.js.org/toolkit/api/combineSlices)

I've since taken a back seat on opening new PRs, as other maintainers have been too busy to review any new changes (and my backlog got very large), but I continue to contribute to discussions.
