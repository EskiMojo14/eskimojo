Tests should say what they mean. mix-n-matchers is a collection of custom
Jest-compatible matchers for making assertions read more like the behavior
they protect.

## Reading the assertion

A useful matcher can communicate intent without making the reader unpack a
chain of transformations first.

This page will eventually show a few examples, including what a failed
assertion tells you and when a built-in matcher is already enough.

## Used on this site

This website's test setup registers mix-n-matchers alongside its browser and
Node tests. That gives this draft a natural place for a real usage example
when the final copy is written.
